# Outlook

## Inserting an emoji in an email

Use the emoji picker:
1. Place cursor in email body
2. Press `<WINDOWS> + .` or `<WINDOWS> + ;` to open emoji panel


## Inserting a table in an email

Be sure that you use the "pop out" button so you're editing the email in 
its own window.  That will give you all the editing options.
